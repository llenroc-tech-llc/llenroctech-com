# Technology logo assets

Brand SVGs in this directory are sourced from the Simple Icons CC0 catalog and rendered in the Llenroc Tech gold accent for consistent contrast. Brand names and marks remain the property of their respective owners and do not imply partnership or endorsement.

Concept SVGs prefixed with `concept-` are Lucide icons (ISC License). They are used when a suitable redistributable vendor mark is unavailable or when the card represents a concept rather than a specific product.

Substitutions:

- OpenAI and OpenAI Agents: `concept-bot`.
- Azure OpenAI, Azure, and AWS cloud concepts: `concept-cloud-cog`; provider names remain in card text.
- LangGraph: LangChain ecosystem mark.
- Pinecone and Weaviate: PostgreSQL/Qdrant plus `concept-database`.
- Langfuse and LangSmith: OpenTelemetry/Grafana plus `concept-workflow`.
- Power BI and general enterprise analytics: Apache Superset/Grafana plus `concept-chart-no-axes-combined`.
- RAG and customer support: `concept-search` and `concept-messages-square`.
