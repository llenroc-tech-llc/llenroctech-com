We use personal information to:

  • Provide, operate, maintain, and deliver our services.

4. SMS and Mobile Messaging Privacy

Mobile information, including phone numbers and SMS consent records, will not be shared, sold, rented, or disclosed to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information may be shared only with service providers that support delivery of the requested messaging service, such as telecommunications providers, and only as necessary to provide that service.

SMS messages may include one-time passcodes, multifactor authentication codes, account verification messages, password-reset messages, security alerts, and service notifications. Message frequency varies based on account activity and user requests. Message and data rates may apply.

You may reply STOP to opt out where applicable or reply HELP for assistance. Opting out of optional SMS messages does not prevent you from completing unrelated primary services, although some security features may require another supported verification method. SMS consent is optional and is not a condition of purchasing unrelated goods or services.

Mobile opt-in information and SMS consent records are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
